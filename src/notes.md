Card types:
    1. Provision (logistic?)
        - gain resorces 
        - gain army
    2. Command (move attack)
        - move army
        - allow for attack, ranged and melee 
    3. Tactics
        - move only
        - ranged attack only
        - harvest
        - bonuses (defence, offence, ...)
    4. Stratagem (instant type card that can be played out of turn)


Turn economy
    1. User can play one type of card per turn.
        - harvest
        - attack
        - bonus 
    
    Turn ORder
        - Game play in broken up into rounds. At the beginning of a round, each player draws 5 cards.
        - Players then take turns playing one at a time.
            - Cards played must follow the 'type' restrictions. Only one command, provision and tactics card may be played per round
        - If a player cannot play a card, they may pass. At this point they may not enter turn order until the next round.
            - A player may buy cards only after they have passed, using their unused cards for the income value. 
        - Players who have not passed, finish the round, skipping any passed players.
        - At the end of the round, pass 'turn marker' to the next player. This player starts the next round.
    Deck managment
        - Cards not played as a command or for their income may be kept and used in the next round. A player draws up to 5 cards.
